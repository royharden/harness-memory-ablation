# Interpretation and construction limitations

Status: runtime-unverified. Read the [metrics contract](metrics/CONTRACT.md) and
[report limitation registry](report/CONTRACT.md#limitations-registry--section-12).

The analysis limitation list and all source records remain visible together.
Rendering neither deduplicates away occurrences nor invents events for missing
slots. Preserve supplied code, raw alias, disposition, affected references,
evidence and unavailable reasons; derived aggregate reasons are not new wire codes.

Reports must retain the union of scientific and operational limitations: authored
class mix, artificial hidden decisions, small Python libraries, four seeds without
run-variance inference, custodian-lineage bias, uncertain backend/model identity,
caps and truncation, partial loader evidence, and generalization limits. Probe
behavior does not prove memory use on every task. Low rot does not establish
successful feature work. Unknown usage suppresses economic comparisons; dated
list prices, harness estimates and actual billing remain distinct.

The generic renderers preserve fields rather than establish scientific validity.
They do not resolve opaque references, verify admission or artifact hashes, inspect
private evidence, or grant execution/publication authority. Upstream analysis must
enforce eligibility and embargoes before results reach rendering. No report source
has been executed or adversarially verified in this construction phase. P0,
independent expected-answer worksheets and K/L acceptance remain deferred.
